About

Suman Sethi is a BS–MS student at IISER Berhampur with a strong interest in mathematics and interdisciplinary scientific research. His work so far spans pure mathematics, mathematical astronomy, and the mathematical analysis of historical scientific literature.

Exploring Sanskrit Literature to Unravel Hidden Scientific Knowledge

An interdisciplinary investigation into mathematical and astronomical knowledge embedded in classical Sanskrit scientific literature.

HighlightReconstructed arithmetic algorithms, trigonometric identities, and planetary computation methods from translated Sanskrit scientific texts spanning approximately 800 BCE–1150 CE, written in rigorous modern mathematical notation across two phases: Mathematics, then Astronomy & Trigonometry. Alongside, designed a structured workflow for recording literature, derivations and references.

Number Theory · Abstract Algebra · Cryptography

Number Theory: The Law of Quadratic Reciprocity

An intensive study of quadratic reciprocity and related results in elementary number theory, with emphasis on rigorous proof construction and computational verification.

HighlightDeveloped a CRT-based proof of the Law of Quadratic Reciprocity, then extended it to related results:
  • Gauss's Lemma
  • The Jacobi symbol and the supplementary laws
  • The Solovay–Strassen primality test
  • The Goldwasser–Micali cryptosystem
Numerical claims were verified with Python and SymPy. A companion applications report links quadratic residue theory to Mersenne number compositeness testing and Paley graph constructions.
